## Hi, I'm Barbara 👋

Postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Vascular Medicine at **Amsterdam UMC**. I study how the gut microbiome relates to cardiovascular health, with a focus on hypertension and on how dietary sodium shapes gut microbial communities and host physiology.

I'm a medical doctor by training, but work as a researcher/bioinformatician. Most of my GitHub is bioinformatics pipelines and analyses of large population-based cohorts with metagenomics data.

## What I work on

- **Gut microbiome and blood pressure**: 16S and shotgun metagenomics, longitudinal cohorts (HELIUS)
- **Salt and the microbiome**: osmoadaptation genes, sodium transporters, and the route from diet to microbes to immune balance to blood pressure
- **Antimicrobial resistance genes** in longitudinal metagenome assemblies
- **Clinical trial analysis**, such as pharmacokinetic modelling and data cleaning for a fructose challenge test
